Lifts: progress per muscle group

Part 3 of the statistics pre-plan (F12: "the By muscle grouping on Lifts can show the same progress per muscle group, with compound exercises counted in full and isolation exercises at half"). It builds on D140's rows, which stay as they are. A group's change is the weighted mean of its lifts' changes over the window: compound lifts count 1, isolation lifts ½. Which is which comes from upstream's name guess with its slips fixed. The count beside it ("2 of 3 rising") is not weighted. A group shows no number until it has two lifts with a change. The groups are D95's: Back covers lats, upper back, lower back and traps, Shoulders covers the three delt heads, and calves and abs are included. Exercise names are yours; every number is made up. Pictures are grey placeholders.

  • By muscle's sections: D95's groups in all three versions, in place of Lifts' six groups (Push, Pull, Legs…), which put squats with calves and biceps with triceps.
  • Recent: unchanged; group progress shows only under By muscle.

A · A header per group Recommended

Each section header carries its group's change and count. The lifts stay under it as today, and an isolation lift has a small ½ mark so you can see why the group's number weighs it less. Nothing moves and nothing is added above the list. T6 (look back at a lift) stays 2 taps.

B · An overview first

By muscle opens with a table of every group: change and count. A group's row jumps to its section, and the sections below have plain headers. You see the whole body at once, but the lifts start below the table.

C · Groups, then their lifts

By muscle lists only the groups, each with its own chart: the weighted change of its lifts, week by week, as an index from 100. A group opens its lifts. The most compact view of the body, but a lift is one tap deeper under By muscle (Recent still opens lifts directly).
