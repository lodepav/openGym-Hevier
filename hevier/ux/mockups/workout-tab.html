The next session: reading the plan and planning weights

The page a routine opens on, from Today's card or from Routines. Three ways to plan the next weights without editing the routine. In all three the page shows what the workout will really open with, last time on the left. Numbers are made up.

A · Plan in place, set by set Approved 2026-11-16 · D128

The page is the plan. A tap on any number changes it for the next session only. It fits how your sets differ, a heavy set and a lighter one, and it works at home or at the gym.

  • The keypad: −2.5 and +2.5 step the weight, "Last time" puts the number back, "Next ›" moves to the next number.
  • The routine itself (its exercises, the number of sets, rest times): "Edit routine" at the top right, as today.
  • Today's card shows the same numbers, so the plan is read without opening this page.
