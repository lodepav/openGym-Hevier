Four proposals from the design critique

2026-11-17, branch hevier/design-fixes. Each proposal shows the screen as it is now, redrawn from the emulator pictures (411 dp; 320 dp for the header), then two or three versions, each scored against the current screen with the before-after lines. Scored from the pictures and the code, not on the emulator: no TalkBack, contrast not measured. Exercise pictures are grey circles. Numbers, names and dates are made up. Where a version changes a decision it says so; you decide.

1 · Two streaks that disagree

Today and Calendar read the fork's streak (D59, frontend/src/hevier/lib/streak.js): weeks from the first to the last trained week, where one week off does not break it and two do; once a run that only two months off break spans a year, it shows in years ("1.4 year streak"). All statistics (History, More) is upstream's card (frontend/src/lib/streak.js, components/Streak.jsx): weeks in a row with at least one workout, where a missed week breaks the run unless a freeze covers it (one freeze per 4 trained weeks, at most 2), with the best run and medals. So after a holiday of two weeks without freezes, one reads 1.4 years and the other 2 weeks, best 61. Both are right; they count different things under one name.

D127 first had a row of effort numbers under the open set, each logging the set in one tap, from your "RPE could replace the tick: why have two".
