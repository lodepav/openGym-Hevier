Round 2, from the owner's notes on round 1: "A, but the History rows stay as they are; at the top are the numbers from B; and take OpenBite's Trends page, where items can be rearranged and switched on and off". So Statistics opens with B's number tiles, then A's cards, and an Arrange Statistics page, as OpenBite's Arrange Trends, sets the order of both and switches each on or off. History is drawn as the fork has it now. Every number is made up; pictures are grey placeholders.

  • History: its three tiles and its workout cards stay as they are. The chips become one row, Calendar · Monthly report · Statistics. Sets per muscle, Body weight and All statistics move onto Statistics.
  • Sets per muscle page: a card on Statistics (by week, month or year, no ranges, D139).
  • Last 7 days body graph: gone from Statistics (rarely looked at; the body map stays on All statistics).
  • Exercise time: a card on Statistics opening its page; its link at the end of Lifts goes. Strength levels stays at the end of Lifts (D140).
  • Body weight chip: a card on Statistics opening Measurements.
  • All statistics and Structural balance: links at the end of Statistics.
