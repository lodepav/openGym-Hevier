## Feature bloat and YAGNI

DECISIONS.md's scope rule: *"New features wait until phases 1–2 work well."* Since then about 30
decisions (D18–D47) have landed in two days.

- **Code to throw away at merge time:** D27 (Health Connect reads), D45 (routine backups), D47
  (location) port upstream 1.4.2/1.4.3, each with "delete at merge" steps. S17, S18, S27 and
  three Java plugins are scheduled to go.
- **Hooks into one upstream file:** `import-hevy.js` and the Hevy import sheet carry 7 hook
  points (S19–S21, S23, S25). Upstream's next import refactor is where merges will hurt.
- **Candidates to cut or freeze** (each adds a field to `S` that syncs forever):
  - D46 learned gym swaps: ~170 lines, a 5-minute "armed" window, silent changes at session
    start. One tap on Replace already covers it.
  - D43 cable setups: a review list, a second `S` map (`hvImportCable`), an import tag syntax.
  - D41 per-exercise unit: display conversion plus rewriting `inc` across routines.
  - D19 strength level: typed-in thresholds for 5 bands.
  - D44 widget: ~280 lines of Java and a midnight alarm.
- **`hevier.css` is 38 KB**, which is large for one theme.
